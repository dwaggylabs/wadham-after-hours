🗺️ Wadham After Hours — Map & Change Planner

A top-down plan of the game world, drawn from the current build. Use it to plan tweaks, then fill the form at the bottom and send the result back to Claude.

How to use this
  1. Read the map. North is up (matches the game). Hover anything for its name, type, position and size.
  2. Get coordinates. Click any empty spot — the position (x, z) appears in the readout (and copies if you hit “copy”). Handy for “add a tree at (12, −40)”.
  3. Plan changes. Scroll to “Changes I want”. Type freely, and/or add structured rows. Hit “📍 pick on map” on a row, then click the map to fill its position.
  4. Send. Click Compile, then Copy (or Download) and paste it back to me. I’ll make the edits.
